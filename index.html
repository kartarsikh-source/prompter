Format preset

Each preset keeps its own text size, line spacing, side margin, reading line height, camera dimming, mirror, dimming of read words, speeds and sound levels. Whatever you change below is saved into the preset you have selected.

Microphone

Plug in your USB receiver first, then pick it here. The level bar at the top shows what it hears. Word-following uses Android's own default mic, so if your USB mic is plugged in, Android normally switches to it automatically.
